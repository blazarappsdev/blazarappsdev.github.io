Money Jar

A small family app for keeping track of kids' money when a parent acts as the bank.

Kids see their balance, their history, and charts of what they earn and spend, and can ask a parent for money. The parent records deposits and withdrawals and approves or declines requests.

All records live in a Google Sheet that the family owns. Money Jar has no server of its own: the app talks directly to Google Sheets on the family's behalf.

Money Jar is built by Blazar Apps for personal family use and is not offered to the general public.
