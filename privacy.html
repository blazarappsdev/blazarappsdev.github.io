Privacy policy

Money Jar · Blazar Apps · Effective October 9, 2026

Money Jar is a family app for tracking kids' allowance and spending. This page explains what data it uses and where that data goes.

Google account and Google Sheets

A parent signs in with Google and grants Money Jar access to Google Sheets. The app uses that access only to read and write the family's own Money Jar spreadsheet: balances, transactions, categories, money requests, kid PINs and settings. That spreadsheet belongs to the family's Google account; Blazar Apps cannot see it.

The sign-in tokens are stored only on the device, in its secure storage, and are sent only to Google. Removing the app, choosing "Disconnect Google account" in Settings, or removing Money Jar at myaccount.google.com/connections ends the app's access.

Push notifications

When a kid sends a money request, the parent's devices get a notification. To do that, each parent device's push token is saved in the family spreadsheet, and the notification (the kid's name, the amount and the reason) is delivered through Expo's push service and then Google Firebase Cloud Messaging or Apple Push Notification service. Those services handle the message only to deliver it.

What Money Jar does not do

  • No accounts or servers run by Blazar Apps; nothing is sent to us.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • No selling, sharing or transferring of data to anyone.
  • No use of Google user data to develop, improve or train AI or machine-learning models.

Google API Services

Money Jar's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

Kids use Money Jar on a device a parent has set up, with a PIN the parent chooses. Kids do not create accounts or give the app any information beyond what their parent records in the family spreadsheet.

Deleting data

All Money Jar data is in the family's spreadsheet. Deleting rows or the spreadsheet in Google Sheets deletes it. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on the device.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date. Questions: blazarappsdev@proton.me.
